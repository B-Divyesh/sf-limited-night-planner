Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026

What the planner stores

Your event details, inventory, notes, timer, and optional archives stay in this browser. An existing Night Pass and its last check result also stay here. We do not receive your event plan. Demo data uses a separate browser space and disappears when you start for real.

Billing and license checks

Restoring an existing Night Pass sends its license token to Sociobot for a check. Sociobot and Dodo process license information under their own policies. They are the merchant of record. Limited Night Planner does not send your plan.

Network and analytics

The app has no advertising, behavioral analytics, trackers, outside fonts, or social embeds. The host may retain normal security logs, such as your address, browser, requested page, and time. The app does not set analytics cookies.

Your control

Export JSON or CSV to keep a copy. “Start over” removes the current plan. You can remove each archive. Clearing this site’s browser data removes plans, archives, and the stored Night Pass.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through sociobot.in.
